How MR AWAY FIX Works

  1. Create an account or sign in.
  2. Customers choose a service and describe the job.
  3. Customers provide the job location and amount.
  4. The service request becomes available to suitable providers.
  5. Providers can respond to available jobs.
  6. The customer reviews provider responses and accepts a provider.
  7. The provider starts and completes the job.
  8. The customer can make the applicable payment through the platform.
  9. The customer can rate and review the provider after the completed service.

Secure Online Payments

MR AWAY FIX supports online payments for completed service jobs through Paystack.

Where a payment is processed through the platform, the payment is recorded against the relevant service request and the applicable provider payment allocation is handled through the platform's payment system.

Payment information is handled through the payment provider rather than stored directly by MR AWAY FIX.

Payment availability depends on the current status of the platform's payment services.

About MR AWAY FIX

MR AWAY FIX is a South African service marketplace designed to connect people who need services with people and businesses offering those services.

The platform is designed to support local service providers by giving them a digital place to present their services and respond to customer requests.

Our vision is to make it easier for customers to find help while creating more opportunities for people with useful skills and services.

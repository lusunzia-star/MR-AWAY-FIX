MR AWAY FIX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

MR AWAY FIX is a South African service marketplace connecting customers with independent service providers.

1. Information collected

Depending on the features used, information may include your name, email address, phone number, account details, service requests, job location, provider profile, messages, reviews and payment references.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to manage accounts, connect customers and providers, coordinate jobs, reconcile payments, respond to complaints, improve platform security and meet legal obligations.

3. Sharing information

Relevant information may be shared with a customer or provider to arrange a job, with payment providers to process transactions, and where reasonably necessary and lawful with service suppliers or authorities.

4. Payment security

MR AWAY FIX should not ask users to send card numbers, banking passwords or account passwords by email. Online payments depend on the approved payment provider and platform configuration.

5. Security and retention

Appropriate measures should be used to protect personal information. Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for legitimate operational, dispute-resolution and legal purposes.

6. Your rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or raise a privacy concern, using the official support contact published on the MR AWAY FIX website. Requests may be subject to identity checks and applicable law.

7. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when data practices change. Before public launch, MR AWAY FIX must confirm that this policy matches the information actually collected and the applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA where applicable, and publish a working privacy contact.
